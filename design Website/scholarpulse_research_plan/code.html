§ 02

Methodological Framework & Dataset Strategy

Our pipeline bifurcates into sequence representation distillation followed by non-Euclidean manifold diffusion. To balance high structural fidelity against conformational variance, we cross-reference static crystallographic data against dynamic ensemble sets.

Equivariant Drift Equation (Formulation 2.1) Continuous Score Matching
dx_t = [ f(x_t, t) - g(t)² ∇_{x_t} log p_t(x_t | C_seq) ] dt + g(t) dw_t
Where C_seq denotes paired MSA embeddings and w_t represents a standard Brownian motion on SE(3) coordinates.
